## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of high-fashion editorial prestige and contemporary circular commerce. It treats second-life fashion not as second-best or purely utilitarian recycling, but as an exclusive, curated archive. The visual tone mirrors the tactile grandeur of a premier fashion biannual: ample negative space, intentional typographic pacing, architectural composition, and disciplined restraint.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Editorial Luxury:** Broad margins, asymmetric white space, deliberate typographic contrast between high-contrast editorial serifs and razor-sharp geometric sans-serifs.
- **Architectural Tactility:** Muted cream surfaces layered over structural taupe borders and warm wine-accented touchpoints rather than stark, tech-minimalist gray scales.
- **Conscious Prestige:** Sustainable circular economy principles communicated through confidence, refined badges, bespoke garment metrics, and timeless editorial styling rather than generic eco-signifiers.

## Colors

The color palette is built on rich, warm, organic tones inspired by vintage textile archives, fine wines, and artisanal paper stocks.

### Hierarchy & Tonal Strategy
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** 
  - Base canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`).
  - Elevated surfaces & card backdrops: Pure Pearl (`#FFFFFF`) and Soft Alabaster (`#FFFDFB`).
  - Recessed backgrounds and subtle dividers: Pressed Linen (`#F5F1EB`) and Warm Beige (`#ECE6DC`).
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:**
  - Headings and prominent text: Deep Obsidian Charcoal (`#1A1918`).
  - Secondary metadata, body labels, and timestamps: Soft Espresso (`#2A2826`) and Muted Bark (`#6B6661`).
  - Structural hairline borders: Soft Taupe (`#D6CEC2`).
- **Accent Systems:**
  - **Primary Wine:** Deep rich burgundy (`#6E1D2D`), fortified by deeper garnet shades (`#541421`) for pressed states and active focal selections.
  - **Secondary Blush:** Dusty blush (`#D9A5B0`) and muted rose mist (`#F7EAEB`) used for secondary highlights, badge fills, and delicate interactive glows.
  - **Tertiary Champagne:** Subtle antique gold (`#D4AF37`) reserved for authentication seals, archival badges, and VIP exclusivity indicators.
  - **Functional Semantic:** Verification/Success anchored in Deep Olive Moss (`#3B5E49`); Pending/Warning anchored in Warm Amber Ochre (`#C27838`).

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges classical bespoke tailoring and razor-sharp digital precision:

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Used exclusively for storytelling, category titling, quotes, designer signatures, and primary section headers. It brings high-contrast serif strokes, evoking traditional mastheads and lookbooks.
- **Operational Interface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Deployed for all transaction-heavy UI, navigation, item listings, metadata, filtering systems, dual pricing matrices, and technical garment specifications.
- **Numeric & Financial Styling:** Dual pricing must display currency markers (`₹`) in regular sans weight, with rental rates in bold medium weights, visually dominating the original retail baseline.
- **Casing Rules:** Category eyebrow tags and tracking tags run in uppercase using `label-sm` or `label-md` with `+0.06em` to `+0.08em` tracking. Display headlines remain in title or sentence case to preserve editorial warmth.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy adapts high-fashion magazine grids to responsive digital viewports, prioritizing rhythm, whitespace, and visual cadence over density.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid bounded at a maximum width of `1440px`. Column gutters maintain `1.5rem` (24px) spacing, flanked by generous `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Breakpoint 768px – 1024px. 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Breakpoint < 768px. 4-column compact fluid structure with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Negative Space
- Sections breathe with larger padding blocks (`space-2xl` on desktop, `space-xl` on mobile) between content blocks to cultivate a relaxed, boutique atmosphere.
- Dense data displays (checkout summaries, garment condition breakdowns) utilize compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments, enclosed within generous structural boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering and soft ambient depth, avoiding synthetic, cold drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Base background tint `#FAF8F5`. Flat, unadorned foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure Pearl `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp `1px` outline tinted with `#D6CEC2` at 60% opacity.
- **Layer 2 (Hover States & Interactive Overlays):** Ambient shadow cast using warm burgundy-tinted darkness: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(110, 29, 45, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Drawers & Sticky Shells):** Subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with ivory translucency (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)`), complemented by an expansive luxury shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural `Soft` geometry (`roundedness: 1`), keeping edge radiuses understated and classic:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Controlled `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) edge softenings maintain a tailored, box-pleated silhouette akin to luxury hangtags and magazine spreads.
- **Pill Tags & Segmented Switchers:** Curated exceptions exist for seasonal status badges, occasion chips, and toggles, which employ full round caps (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast against rectilinear image containers.
- **Image Ratios:** Strict vertical proportions—primarily 3:4 portrait or 4:5 editorial crops—to honor garment drape, full-length silhouettes, and runway photography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Burgundy Action:** Solid `#6E1D2D` background, text in `#FFFFFF`, with `0.25rem` corner radius. Subtle hover transitions to `#541421`. Horizontal padding `1.75rem`, vertical padding `0.875rem`. Text styled in `label-lg` uppercase with wide tracking.
- **Secondary Champagne/Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px` solid `#1A1918`, text in `#1A1918`. Transitions to `#FAF8F5` background on hover.
- **Tertiary Ghost/Text:** Clean text button in `#6E1D2D` with an animated hairline underline (`#6E1D2D`) that expands from center on hover.

### Chips & Segmented Controls
- **Occasion & Filter Chips:** Full-pill shaped tags (`rounded-full`) in `#FFFFFF` with `#D6CEC2` 1px border. Selected state transitions to `#6E1D2D` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Luxury Segmented Control:** Encapsulated within a `#ECE6DC` container with `4px` padding. Active pill lifts slightly with `#FFFFFF` background and ambient shadow, styled in `label-md`.

### Outfit Cards
- **Structure:** 3:4 aspect ratio image container topped by an overlay pill for occasion tags (e.g., "Black Tie", "Sangeet Edit") in the top-left, and a floating circular wishlist heart button (`#FFFFFF` with soft backdrop blur) in the top-right.
- **Condition & Trust Tag:** Displayed below the image alongside the designer/brand line using `label-sm` with a moss green dot (`#3B5E49`) indicating verified pristine condition.
- **Dual Pricing Badge:** Arranged on two horizontal tiers:
  - *Rental Price:* Prominently emphasized using `title-md` in `#6E1D2D` (e.g., "₹2,400 / day").
  - *Retail Comparison:* Muted struck-through pricing in `body-sm` `#6B6661` (e.g., "Retail ₹28,000") to immediately demonstrate circular economy value.

### Occasion Cards
- High-contrast visual modules featuring edge-to-edge ambient photography veiled with a gradual gradient overlay (`linear-gradient(to top, rgba(26,25,24,0.85) 0%, rgba(26,25,24,0) 60%)`).
- Headings set in `headline-md` Playfair Display in white, accompanied by an editorial counter indicating active rental pieces.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Floating label style with bottom-only borders (`1.5px` solid `#D6CEC2`) on `#FFFFFF` surfaces, transitioning to `#6E1D2D` on focus with no loud glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted square checks with `2px` rounding, filling with `#6E1D2D` and an ivory checkmark when selected.

### Sticky Navigation Header
- Frosted ivory surface (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.92)` backdrop blur `12px`) with an ultra-thin bottom divider (`#ECE6DC`).
- Centered brand masthead set with refined serif authority, flanked by streamlined category links in `label-md` and burgundy CTA buttons ("Borrow", "Lend / Consign").
